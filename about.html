About Rainy Days

Rainy Days is a Scandinavian outdoor brand dedicated to creating durable, waterproof jackets designed for everyday adventures. We believe that great outdoor clothing should protect you from the elements without compromising on style. Whether you are exploring the city, hiking in nature, or commuting in the rain, Rainy Days jackets are built to keep you warm, dry, and comfortable.

Our focus is on quality materials, practical features, and timeless designs that last season after season. Rainy Days is made for those who embrace the rain and make the most of every day.
